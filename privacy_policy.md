# FitPulse Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

FitPulse (“we”, “us”) provides a fitness and nutrition tracking app. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your choices. Contact: fitpulsesupport@gmail.com.

## 1. Who we are

FitPulse provides tools to log nutrition, workouts, water intake, and body metrics, including optional AI meal-photo estimates, barcode lookup, reminders, health-data sync where supported, and optional FitPulse Pro subscriptions for higher daily meal-scan limits. Contact: fitpulsesupport@gmail.com.

## 2. Data we collect

Depending on how you use FitPulse, we may process: account details (email, display name, authentication identifiers from Google or Apple); profile and goals (age, gender, height, weight, target weight, fitness and diet preferences, calorie and macro targets, intermittent fasting windows you set); health and activity logs you enter (meals, workouts, water, body metrics); optional meal photos you choose to scan (sent to Nebius Token Factory running Qwen vision/language models to estimate nutrition — used to generate an estimate you confirm before logging, and not used to train our own models); optional device health data you authorize (for example step counts via Apple Health or Health Connect); subscription and usage data (plan, status, product identifiers, store, period dates, transaction identifiers needed to restore purchases, and daily meal-scan usage counts); optional on-device biometric unlock (Face ID / fingerprint — biometrics stay on your device and are not sent to our servers); device and diagnostics data needed for crash reporting, analytics, and app reliability where enabled; and support messages you send us. Payment card details for subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google; we do not receive your full payment card number.

## 3. How we use data

We use your data to provide and sync the App, calculate personalized calorie and macro targets, process optional meal-photo estimates, enforce free and Pro meal-scan limits, manage subscriptions you purchase, send local or push reminders you enable, improve reliability and fix bugs, and respond to support requests. We do not sell your personal information.

## 4. Storage and sync

Logs may be stored on your device (including offline cache) and, when you are signed in, synced to cloud services such as Firebase (Google) so your data is available across devices. Security measures include encrypted transit and access controls appropriate to the service.

## 5. Third-party services

We use providers that process data on our behalf or at your request, including Firebase (authentication, database, storage, analytics/crash reporting where enabled), Open Food Facts (food search and barcode lookup — queries may be sent to their servers), Nebius Token Factory / Qwen (optional meal-photo nutrition estimates), Apple App Store and Google Play (subscription billing and restore), and Apple Health / Google Health Connect when you grant permission. Their privacy policies apply to their processing.

## 6. Your choices and rights

You can update profile data in the App, export logs from Settings where available (PDF), manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple or Google account settings, revoke health or camera permissions in system settings, turn off biometric unlock in the App, and delete your account in Settings (which removes associated cloud profile, logs, and subscription entitlement records subject to backup and legal retention; store billing history remains with Apple or Google). Depending on your region, you may have additional rights (access, correction, deletion, portability). Contact fitpulsesupport@gmail.com to exercise them.

## 7. Children

FitPulse is not directed to children under 16 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children below that age.

## 8. International transfers

If you use the App outside the country where our processors operate, your data may be transferred and processed in other countries with different data-protection laws. We rely on appropriate safeguards offered by our service providers where required.

## 9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use after updates means you acknowledge the revised policy. Material changes may be highlighted in the App.

## 10. Contact

Privacy questions: fitpulsesupport@gmail.com
