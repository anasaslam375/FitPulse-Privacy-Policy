# FitPulse Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

FitPulse (“we”, “us”) provides a fitness and nutrition tracking app. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your choices. Contact: fitpulsesupport@gmail.com.

## 1. Who we are

FitPulse provides tools to log nutrition, workouts, water intake, and body metrics. Contact: fitpulsesupport@gmail.com.

## 2. Data we collect

Depending on how you use FitPulse, we may process: account details (email, display name, authentication identifiers from Google or Apple); profile and goals (age, gender, height, weight, fitness and diet preferences, calorie and macro targets); health and activity logs you enter (meals, workouts, water, body metrics); optional meal photos you choose to scan (sent to Google Gemini / Generative Language API to estimate nutrition, then not used to train our own models); optional device health data you authorize (for example step counts via Apple Health or Health Connect); device and diagnostics data needed for crash reporting and app reliability; and support messages you send us.

## 3. How we use data

We use your data to provide and sync the App, calculate personalized calorie and macro targets, send local or push reminders you enable, improve reliability and fix bugs, and respond to support requests. We do not sell your personal information.

## 4. Storage and sync

Logs may be stored on your device (including offline cache) and, when you are signed in, synced to cloud services such as Firebase (Google) so your data is available across devices. Security measures include encrypted transit and access controls appropriate to the service.

## 5. Third-party services

We use providers that process data on our behalf or at your request, including Firebase (authentication, database, storage, analytics/crash reporting where enabled), Open Food Facts (food search and barcode lookup — queries may be sent to their servers), Google Gemini (optional meal-photo nutrition estimates), and Apple Health / Google Health Connect when you grant permission. Their privacy policies apply to their processing.

## 6. Your choices and rights

You can update profile data in the App, export logs from Settings where available, revoke health permissions in system settings, and delete your account in Settings (which removes associated cloud profile and logs subject to backup and legal retention). Depending on your region, you may have additional rights (access, correction, deletion, portability). Contact fitpulsesupport@gmail.com to exercise them.

## 7. Children

FitPulse is not directed to children under 16 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children below that age.

## 8. International transfers

If you use the App outside the country where our processors operate, your data may be transferred and processed in other countries with different data-protection laws. We rely on appropriate safeguards offered by our service providers where required.

## 9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use after updates means you acknowledge the revised policy. Material changes may be highlighted in the App.

## 10. Contact

Privacy questions: fitpulsesupport@gmail.com
